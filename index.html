    Golang Roguelike Tutorial

    This is one program, grown one small step at a time into a complete, classic terminal roguelike in Go with tcell. Step 1 is ten lines. Every step after it adds a handful of lines, shows you exactly which ones, and explains each new piece of Go the first time you meet it: defer, methods, interfaces, embedding, closures, container/heap, encoding/gob... By the end you have written all ~2,300 lines yourself and you know why every one of them is there.

    • About 50 steps in 14 chapters, each a few lines with the reason for them.
    • Each step starts from something you can see go wrong, then asks you to try it first: load the previous step into the editor, make it do the new thing, play it, then reveal how the step does it.
    • The exact lines to add, as a diff against the previous step, with every new piece of Go explained. The whole program is always one tab away.
    • Break it on purpose experiments that show why a line is there.
    • Play here: the server builds the step, with your edits, and runs it in a terminal in the page or in your own terminal window.
    • An exercise at the end of every chapter, checked by hidden Go tests.
    • The kobold in the corner keeps your progress, in this browser only.

    Every step is also a plain Go program you can run yourself from a clone of this repo:

    go run ./steps/01-hello-tcell

    You need Go 1.22+ and, for your own terminal, at least 80×50 characters. Pick any step from the list, or follow them in order.
